# The World Today — 25 November 2026

One image that sums up Wednesday 25 November 2026's world news, drawn as a
newspaper front page: a masthead, the date in a red band, and six illustrated
story cards across regions and fields.

1. **CIA & ODNI hacked** — an unclassified intel system was hit; officials
   say no classified data was affected (CNN / The New York Times).
2. **NATO sends AWACS to Poland** — early-warning jets for the eastern flank
   after Russian missiles landed in Poland (Stars and Stripes / Reuters).
3. **Ukraine hit Russian ship** — a drone struck the cargo ship Sparta II in
   the Gulf of Finland, confirmed by President Zelensky (Reuters).
4. **New US tariffs coming** — Trump set to unveil duties on 16 trading
   partners after an excess-capacity probe (Reuters / Bloomberg).
5. **WHO evacuates Ebola hospital** — staff left a hospital in DR Congo after
   three days sheltering from fighting (WHO).
6. **England level T20 series** — an eight-wicket win over Australia in the
   2nd T20I, 1-1 (match reports).

The image is drawn from scratch (PIL shapes and text, no photos or borrowed
artwork) and copied to `/output/entry.png` by `main.ts` (Node 22 stdlib only).
